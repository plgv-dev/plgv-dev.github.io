FAQ

Questions customers may have.

How do I start a project?

Send an enquiry through WhatsApp or email. Tell us what you need and we'll discuss the requirements before providing a quotation.

Do you offer website hosting?

Yes. We offer website hosting and setup support. The exact arrangement depends on the website, domain, technology and hosting requirements.

What do you mean by Google Optimization?

We can discuss search-visibility work such as website structure, page titles and descriptions, content organization, technical basics and business information. Search engines control their own rankings, so no specific ranking position is guaranteed.

Can you create a website and graphics for the same business?

Yes. Website design, hosting, graphic design, branding and promotional materials can be combined into a broader business-presence project.

Do you work with businesses outside Kasoa?

Yes. Many digital projects can be discussed and delivered remotely. Contact us with your requirements.

Do you provide a fixed price immediately?

For custom work, the quotation depends on the requirements. We prefer to understand the scope first so the price is clear before work begins.

Can you add new services later?

Yes. Prince Link Global Ventures is intentionally structured as an umbrella brand so new services and ventures can be added over time.
